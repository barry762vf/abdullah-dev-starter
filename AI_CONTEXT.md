# Repository context and optional knowledge-base bridge

This reusable starter keeps its technical source of truth in code, accepted `.ai/DECISIONS.md` records, `.ai/` current state, and `docs/`, in that order. The `.ai/` directory preserves collaboration and handoff history for engineers and coding agents.

An owner may keep a private Obsidian vault or other long-term knowledge base. Its path, profile, project history, credentials and device details belong in private configuration, outside this template. No vault is needed to build, test, or deploy this repository. Read only relevant private notes when the owner explicitly makes them available.

After a meaningful milestone, `SECOND_BRAIN_HANDOFF.md` may carry a short durable summary: the milestone, important architecture decisions, lessons, major unresolved issues, and next direction. Keep commands, raw logs, and day-to-day implementation details in `.ai/` instead.
